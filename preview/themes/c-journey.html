Three products, research to MVP

GGroww line, first-time mutual fund investors

Why first-year SIPs stop, and a passbook built to keep them going

  1. Question

    Why do first-time investors stop their SIP in the first year?

  2. Evidence

    A discovery engine over public posts, narrowed step by step.

    1. Public records1,631,157
    2. Episodes analysed90,755
    3. Stop stories684
  3. Insight

    Losses make people think about stopping. Payment lapses (35% of stop stories) and app friction (30%) make them stop.

  4. Shipped

    SIP Passbook tracks every instalment like an online order. Its AI explainer passed 18 of 20 test questions with no critical errors.

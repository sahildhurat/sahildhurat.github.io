Groww, first-time mutual fund investors

Why first-year SIPs stop, and a passbook built to keep them going

Losses make new investors think about stopping. Failed payments and unanswered "where is my money" questions make them actually stop: payment lapses appear in 35% of stop stories, disappointing returns in 21%.

I built SIP Passbook, which tracks every instalment like an online order. Its AI explainer passed 18 of 20 hand-labelled test questions with no critical errors.
